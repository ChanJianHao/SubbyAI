# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Interruptible page transitions, sliding settings and transcript panels,
  animated switches, tab indicators, focus rings and press feedback.
- Responsive Mochi expressions, smooth audio meters and progress, bounded busy
  indicators and clearer copy/save/connection confirmations.

## 1.0.0

First SubbyAI release.

- Live original-language captions with independent local or optional remote translation.
- System-audio and microphone modes with source-aware defaults, device recovery and setup.
- Simple settings, Advanced Mode and hardware-aware Fast/Balanced/Accurate/Maximum Quality profiles.
- Skippable setup with hardware recommendations and a real audio meter.
- Original Mochi artwork, Sakura/Ocean accents and light/dark/system themes.
- Eight built-in subtitle looks plus Custom, saved personal looks, separate text colours,
  borders, always-on-top control and configurable display placement.
- Unicode-aware wrapping, installed-font selection, labelled controls and larger app text.
- Revision-pinned speech models, validated language packs and cancellable downloads.
- Consent-based compatible speech servers and endpoint-bound OS credential storage.
- Opt-in original/translation/session-only storage, searchable history, exports and assistance.
- Conservative CPU allocation and configurable idle model release.
- Bounded queues, visible overload diagnostics and graceful device/failure recovery.
- Per-user Windows installer, portable distribution and architecture-specific macOS bundles.

V1 binaries are unsigned; installation guides explain the platform prompts.
